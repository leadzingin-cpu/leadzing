## Digital PR — link-worthy assets to build

Directories give you citations. These earn genuine editorial links. **This is where the real authority comes from.**

### Asset 1 — "State of Marketing for Kolkata SMBs" (original research)
Survey 100–200 local businesses on marketing spend, channels, and pain points. Publish as a free report with charts.

**Why it works:** original data is the most reliably linked-to content type. Regional business journalism has almost no local data to cite. You'd become the source.
**Effort:** High · **Value:** Highest · **Fabrication risk:** None — it's your own survey.

### Asset 2 — Free interactive tool
Options: marketing budget calculator, brand audit scorecard, website speed grader.

**Why it works:** tools earn passive links for years. Fits the "AI Automation" positioning and demonstrates the web-development capability.
**Effort:** Medium · **Value:** High

### Asset 3 — Founder commentary
Owais Raza as a named source on Indian SMB marketing. Pitch to regional business press and marketing publications.

**Why it works:** builds the `Person` entity already in the schema graph, and links a real human to the brand. Strongest possible EEAT signal.
**Effort:** Low-medium · **Value:** High

### Asset 4 — Podcast appearances
Indian marketing/startup podcasts. Show notes almost always link back.
**Effort:** Low · **Value:** Medium

## Sequencing

1. **Week 1:** GBP + Bing Places + LinkedIn *(blocked on street address)*
2. **Week 2:** Clutch, GoodFirms, Crunchbase — start review collection on Clutch
3. **Week 3:** Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Startup India
4. **Week 4:** Behance/Dribbble portfolio submissions
5. **Month 2+:** Begin Asset 1 or 2. This is the compounding work.

**Do not chase volume.** Twenty relevant citations plus one genuinely cited research asset will outperform two hundred directory links.
